Project Overview

Real-time monitoring of water quality parameters

About the Project

The Real-Time Water Quality Analysis System is a mini project focused on monitoring important water quality parameters using sensors and a microcontroller-based data acquisition system.


The system uses a pH sensor and turbidity sensor connected to an Arduino UNO. The collected readings are intended for digital monitoring and analysis.

Problem Statement

Need for digital water quality monitoring

Water Quality Monitoring

Water quality can vary depending on different environmental and usage conditions. This project explores a sensor-based approach for obtaining water quality readings and presenting them digitally for easier monitoring and analysis.
